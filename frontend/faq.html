FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Still have a question?

We’re happy to help you find the right answer for your space.

Contact Us
01Can I get help choosing furniture?

Yes. Tell us about your room, measurements, and preferences, and our team can help you explore suitable options.

02How can I check if a piece fits my space?

Measure the room and the path into it, then share those dimensions with us. We can help you compare them with a piece’s width, depth, and height.

03How do I check delivery availability?

Delivery options depend on your location and the items in your order. Contact us with your area and the products you are considering.

04Can I ask about materials or dimensions?

Absolutely. Get in touch with the product name and we’ll help with the available specifications, finishes, and care information.

05What if I need help after purchasing?

Contact our team with your order details and question so we can point you to the right support.
